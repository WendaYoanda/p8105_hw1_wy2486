The `penguins` dataset contains information on penguins from three
species: Adelie, Gentoo, and Chinstrap, observed on the Torgersen,
Biscoe, and Dream islands. Important variables include species, island,
bill length, bill depth, flipper length, body mass, sex, and year.

The dataset contains 344 observations and 8 variables.

The mean flipper length is 200.92 mm.
